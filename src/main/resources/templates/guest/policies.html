Terms of Service

Guests must present a valid government-issued ID and credit card at check-in.

All reservations are subject to verification. The hotel reserves the right to cancel bookings if guest details cannot be verified.

Guests are responsible for any damage caused to hotel property during their stay.

Smoking is prohibited in all guest rooms. A cleaning fee will be charged for violations.

Pets are not allowed on hotel premises unless otherwise specified in the booking.

The hotel is not liable for lost or stolen personal belongings.

Cancellation & Refunds

Standard Cancellation: Free cancellations are allowed up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled check-in date. Cancellations made inside the 24-hour window will forfeit the booking deposit.

Refund Policy: Eligible refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.

No-Show Policy: In case of a no-show, the reservation will be held until 23:59 on the check-in date, after which it will be cancelled and the entire deposit will be forfeited.
